Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

MEI Velocity provides ride-booking and related transport services in Zimbabwe. This policy explains how information is used when you use our customer app and website.

1. Information we collect

We collect account details such as username and phone number; booking details including pickup and destination addresses, ride time, distance, fare and payment method; location coordinates when you use location-based ride features; ride messages, ratings and service-related technical information. Passwords are stored as hashes rather than plaintext. Notification subscriptions or device push tokens may be processed when notifications are enabled.

2. How we use information

We use this information to create and secure accounts, verify phone numbers, process bookings, connect customers with drivers, show ride progress, calculate estimated fares, send service notifications, provide support and maintain operational records.

3. Location information

Location access helps identify pickup points, show relevant map information and support driver coordination. You may enter pickup details manually where supported. During an active ride, location updates may be shared with the assigned driver and the service systems necessary to fulfil the booking. Location permissions can be managed in device settings.

4. Sharing and service providers

Booking and location details may be shared with assigned drivers as necessary to deliver the service. We use technical service providers for hosting, databases, SMS verification and notifications. These providers process relevant data to deliver their services. We do not sell personal information.

5. Data security

We use authentication controls and password hashing to protect accounts. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention and account deletion

You may request account deletion through the app's account deletion feature. Active rides must first be completed or cancelled. When an account is deleted, associated account details and ride messages are removed; cancelled bookings are deleted. Completed ride records may be retained for operational accounting, with customer identity, addresses and precise location fields removed. Other records may be retained where required by applicable law.

7. Your choices and rights

You can manage location and notification permissions in device settings and request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information by contacting support, subject to applicable legal requirements.

8. Children

The service is not intended for children to use independently. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate authorization.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as our services change. The current version will be posted here with its updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact MEI Velocity support at +49 1529 2617715.
