Privacy Policy

This policy explains the information MEI Velocity uses to provide ride-booking services and the choices available to customers.

Effective date: 16 October 2026

1. Who we are

MEI Velocity is a ride-booking platform serving customers and drivers. For privacy-related questions or requests, contact MEI Velocity using the details in Section 10.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use our services, information processed may include:

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number (if provided), sign-in identifiers, and account verification information.
  • Ride details: pickup and destination information, requested services, booking times, ride status, and booking history.
  • Location information: precise device location when you grant location access, and location information you enter manually for pickups or destinations.
  • Communications: messages exchanged through in-app features and communications sent to our support channels.
  • Technical information: information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and deliver the app, such as device or app diagnostics where available.

You may decline device location permission and enter an address manually where that option is supported. Some features may not work as intended without location access.

3. How we use information

  • Create and manage customer accounts, authenticate sign-ins, and send verification messages.
  • Calculate or display routes, process ride requests, and coordinate pickups and trips.
  • Share relevant booking and pickup information with a driver assigned to your request.
  • Display ride status and driver location when an active driver tracking feature is available.
  • Support rider–driver communication and send relevant service notifications.
  • Respond to support requests, maintain service reliability, and investigate potential misuse.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Location access

MEI Velocity uses location information to help identify pickup points, show maps, and support ride coordination. Location is requested through your device's permission controls. You can change permission settings at any time in your device settings. If a feature needs ongoing or background location access, the app should request the corresponding permission and explain why before using it.

5. When information is shared

We may share information required to provide the service with relevant drivers, technical service providers (such as hosting, mapping, messaging, or notification providers), and authorities when required by applicable law. Information shared with drivers should be limited to what is needed to manage the requested ride. We do not describe customer information as sold to advertisers.

6. Storage, retention, and security

Information may be stored by MEI Velocity and the service providers used to operate the app. We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, manage legitimate operational requirements, resolve disputes, or comply with legal duties. Retention periods can vary by type of record and applicable law. We take reasonable measures to protect information; however, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your privacy choices and requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us. We will consider and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law, including any valid retention requirements. You can also manage location and notification permissions through your device settings. To request account deletion, contact support and identify the account you want removed; we may need to verify that the request is authorised.

8. Children's privacy

MEI Velocity is not intended for children to use independently where they are not legally permitted to use ride-booking services. If you believe a child has supplied personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the situation.

9. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, data practices, or legal requirements change. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
