MEI VELOCITY PRIVACY PAGE UPDATE

1. Extract privacy.html into your website ROOT (the same directory as index.html).
2. Open backend/server.js.
3. In the PUBLIC_FILES Set near the bottom, add "privacy.html" to the list.
   Example: "index.html", "privacy.html", "admin.html", ...
4. Commit and push the changed files to GitHub, then wait for Render deployment.
5. Test: https://mei-velocity1.onrender.com/privacy.html
6. Paste that URL into the Google Play Console Privacy policy field.
7. Add a link to privacy.html from your website About page or footer.

IMPORTANT: Review the policy for accuracy before publishing, especially providers,
data retention, contact information and whether you collect analytics or crash reports.
If your app uses third-party mapping, geocoding or analytics services, disclose them
accurately. This policy is a starting draft, not a legal compliance certification.
